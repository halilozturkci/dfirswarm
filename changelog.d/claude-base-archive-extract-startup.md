### Fixed: computer-forensics-base `archive_extract` hashes and asks 7z for its banner on the run's clock, and stops from the start

- **The archive hash and the 7z banner are on the run's clock.** The hash (up to 4 GiB) and `7z`'s banner ran before the clock started, so with `max_seconds` at its ceiling a medium slower than about 40 MB/s took the run past the manifest's 1,800 seconds. The clock now starts with the tool; the hash reads it for every block, opens the archive without blocking and checks on the descriptor that it is a regular file, and a hash the clock cut short is `sha256_skipped` with the reason ("the time budget ran out after N bytes of the archive were hashed"); the banner gets only the time left. A resume still compares digests only when both runs have one.
- **The stop handlers are in from the PROCESS block on.** They were installed in `main()`, after the arguments were read and the archive was detected, so a signal before that killed the tool with no answer. They are now installed at module level right after the block with a minimal last word (`status: failed`, `interrupted: true`, the signal, exit 128 plus the signal); `main()` replaces the last word with its own once the engine exists. The start of the interpreter, before any handler, is not claimed. Arguments nested past the parser are refused as JSON.
