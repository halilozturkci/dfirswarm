### Fixed: computer-forensics-base `chunk_needles` keeps every needle's locators in one file and says when a list was lost

- **One locator file for every needle.** A needle that went past its inline page used to get a temporary file of its own, so a call with many such needles opened as many files at once and ran into the open-file limit (about a thousand needles near a limit of 1,024), and the lists it could not open were lost. The locators of every needle past its page are now written to one JSON Lines file whose rows name their needle (`needle_index`, the position in the needles given with a repeated one dropped, and `needle`, its shown form up to 120 characters); each needle's entry in `hits` gives `needle_index`, `all_results` (the file) and `all_results_rows`. The byte cap (1 GiB) and the row cap (20,000,000) now apply to the file: when it is reached no row of any needle is written from that offset on, and a needle that is short of its rows says so (`all_results_complete: false`).
- **A list that is not whole makes the answer `partial`.** A locator file that could not be created, a write that failed (a full disk), or a cap that was reached used to leave `status: complete` and `status_basis` saying every occurrence was reported, with the loss only in `all_results_error`. The answer is now `partial` with `complete: false`, and `status_basis` names the needles (`needle_index`) and what to do. A file that failed is not kept half written; the counts and the inline pages are of the whole scan.
- **The locator and values directories are made by an own-directory walk** (the `own_dir` of `pe_directories`): a symbolic link on the way, at `work/`, at the agent's directory, at `tool-output` or at `$OUT`, is refused and nothing is written through it; for the locators the answer says so (`all_results_error`) and is partial, for `write_values` the call is refused as JSON.
- **Refusals carry `status: failed` and `status_basis`**, arguments nested past the parser and a `path` that is not text are refused as JSON, and an error nobody expected answers `failed` with the error's class and no message.
