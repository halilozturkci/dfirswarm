### Fixed: computer-forensics-base `feature_scan` runs bulk_extractor through the shared PROCESS block and counts on a clock

- **bulk_extractor runs through the general PROCESS block** (the text `archive_extract` carries): in the tool's own process group, with `PR_SET_PDEATHSIG` on Linux, and the tree below it ended at the deadline and on a stop; the answer carries `process_notes` when the block could not do something (a tree it could not list). A program that cannot be started (not executable, a script with no interpreter line) is a JSON `failed` that names the program, no longer a traceback, and leaves no hidden file beside `out_dir`.
- **SIGTERM, SIGINT and SIGHUP give one document.** The handlers are in from the block on, with a minimal last word until `main()` has the arguments (`failed`, `interrupted`, exit 128 plus the signal); after that the last word ends bulk_extractor and what it started, restricts the modes of what it had written (0700 and 0600, as after a run that ends by itself), removes the hidden stdout and stderr files and answers `partial` with `interrupted: true`. The start of the interpreter is not claimed.
- **Counting what bulk_extractor wrote is on a clock** that ends at 1,150 seconds, below the manifest's 1,200 (restricting the modes ends at 1,180). Feature and histogram files are read in blocks of at most 1 MiB with the clock read before each, so one very long line cannot hold the run (`lines_longer_than_a_block` counts them), and a file is opened without blocking and checked to be a regular file. The walk of `out_dir` reads the clock for every entry and looks at no more than 100,000 entries of a directory. What the clock cut short is named (`not_counted`, with its total) and the status is `partial`, with a `status_basis` that says bulk_extractor exited 0 and the counting did not finish. Feature files and histograms are listed up to 200 each, with `feature_count` and `histogram_count`; `out_dir` holds them all.
- **Refusals carry `status: failed` and `status_basis`**, and arguments nested past the parser, a NUL in `path` or `out_dir`, and a lone surrogate are refused as JSON.
