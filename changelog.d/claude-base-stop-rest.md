### Fixed: computer-forensics-base `ad1_extract`, `file_type`, `image_layout`, `timestamp_decode`, `sqlite_snapshot` and the `ad1-items`, `archive-members` and `disk-timeline` recipes answer once on a stop signal

- **SIGTERM, SIGINT and SIGHUP give one JSON document, exit 128 plus the signal, and leave no hidden temporary file.** The four tools without a handler and the three Python recipes now carry the shared STOP block (the windows lineage, byte for byte) at module level right after the standard imports; `sqlite_snapshot`'s own handler, installed after it had made its output directory, is gone. Until the work has begun a stop is `failed` with `interrupted: true`; once it has, it is `partial` with `interrupted: true`, the signal and what was done. A recipe writes its coverage as `partial` and `interrupted` first (`ad1-items`, `archive-members`, `disk-timeline`); `ad1_extract` writes `ad1_extract.coverage.json` the same way, with `integrity_status: unverified`.
- **A file that was being written is not left under a name that says it is whole.** `ad1_extract` renames the item it was writing to `<name>.partial` and names it in the answer (`partial_file`); `icat_extract` already kept its `.partial`; `sqlite_snapshot` names the `.partial` copies it leaves (`partial_files`) and its receipt says `status: partial` with `interrupted: true` (it said `interrupted` as a status, which the contract does not have).
- **The program a tool or recipe runs is ended by its pid.** `file_type`'s `file`, `image_layout`'s Sleuth Kit programs, `archive-members`' `7z` and `disk-timeline`'s Plaso programs are started so that their pid is known, and the last word ends them before it answers; the tests look at the pids.
- **`catalog_search` no longer carries an image resolver it never called.** `_resolve_image`, `_inputs_roots` and `_catalogue_slug` were dead code, and the first walked `inputs/` with `os.walk` and no clock; they are removed.
- The final answers go through the block's `print_answer`, so a signal during the write cannot cut the document. A signal in the start of the interpreter, before the block has run, is not claimed.
