### Fixed: computer-forensics-base

- **verify_sources is inside the tool's own clock.** `chunk_needles`, `image_layout`, `icat_extract` and the `disk-volumes` recipe gave the check of a descriptor's sources a clock of 120 seconds of its own, on top of the time budget the tool then started: a 120 GiB sparse source answered after 120.06 s against a manifest timeout of 120 s, and `icat_extract` could add 120 s to 1700. The tools now count their budget from the start of the process and give the verify the time that is left, never more than 120 seconds (the recipe: what its own clock has left); a verify that runs out ends the run `failed` with JSON and the words `time budget`. `tests/test_verify_budget.py` does it with a sparse source of 64 GiB for all four, and fails on the earlier code.
- **byte_range_extract** has no environment hook for the tests any more (the stop test copies a sparse range of 4 GiB and signals it at the first bytes); its manifest says what `out_dir` takes (absent, or holding an earlier run, which nothing replaces); two tests hold a copy still (SIGSTOP) while the source changes, and while its final name is taken: the run is `failed`, and the name is not replaced.
- **image_verify** makes the receipt `partial` and says so when `ewfverify -V` does not answer or prints nothing (the version was null with the receipt `complete`); the manifest's `max_seconds` is 5 to 3300, as the code takes it.
- **filesystem/layers** says `image_layout` reads one path, or the descriptor.
- **The twins follow the pack.** `tool-library/icat_extract` and `tool-library/chunk_needles` carry the pack's resealed manifests again (the `run.py` was copied and the manifests were not), and the register of sentences that must stay in `filesystem/layers` names the reworded one (`image_layout` reads one path, or the descriptor). computer-forensics-base 1.14.5.
