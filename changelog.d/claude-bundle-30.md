### Added: the macOS pack's `macos_tcc`, `macos_login_items`, `safari_history`, `segb_read` and `apfs_inventory`

- **`macos_tcc`** reads one system or user `TCC.db` through a private copy of the database and its `-wal`, `-shm` and `-journal` (the WAL applied there, a read-only authorizer, the copy removed; a sidecar that is a link or a FIFO is named and not followed, and the answer is partial). The columns of the schema found are shown raw (`PRAGMA table_info`, the `admin` version row recorded); only `auth_value` 0 to 3 and `client_type` 0 and 1 are decoded, with the raw number beside; `last_modified` and `last_reminder` are Unix seconds given as UTC and called the row's last write, never a grant time; `managed` is true when `policy_id` is set; the code-signing requirement is its length only. Tests use fixtures of the `access` table of macOS 10.15, 11, 12, 13, 14 and 15 (26 and 27 are not built: their schema is not verified here).
- **`macos_login_items`** resolves `backgrounditems.btm` and `BackgroundItems-v*.btm` keyed archives into items, with `reference_path` (the hops from `$top`) and `via_objects` for every property, the archive's state fields raw, NSURL, NSUUID and NSDate resolved, and data (a bookmark or an alias) as present with its length only. A dangling reference, a cycle, the depth bound and the node bound are rows with a reason and make the answer partial; the walk is linear in the objects. A store that is not a keyed archive, or holds no item, is named and partial, never an empty complete answer. It does not replace `plist_read`, which stays the structural view.
- **`safari_history`** reads Safari's `History.db` through a private copy (visits only in the `-wal` are read): one entry per visit with every column raw, the Apple-absolute REAL time beside its UTC reading (read as Unix time it lands in 1970), URL query values withheld by shape, and the whole URL written only with `write_values` in a job run as secret output (the manifest declares `secret_output` for it).
- **`segb_read`** decodes the framing of SEGB1 and SEGB2 files (frame offsets, state, the two container times), answers `unsupported` for an unknown version, fails a layout whose bounds do not fit instead of decoding it by luck, and shows a payload only as protobuf tags with the confidence stated. The layouts are from public reverse-engineering notes and are not checked against Apple's own or against a sample file in this repository; every answer says so. `artifacts/knowledgec` names it where it said the pack had no decoder.
- **`apfs_inventory`** runs `fsapfsinfo` (version recorded in every answer) in its own process group under the shared process block, keeps its stdout and stderr whole on disk, and reads the container and volume sections by their labels. Every answer says no snapshot is enumerated, no object is extracted and no password is passed; a missing reader, a non-zero exit, a hang, a flood, an output with no container section or a refused write is a failed or partial answer with the reason.

`persistence/login-items-quieter`, `artifacts/knowledgec`, `artifacts/plists-archives-dates` and `filesystem/apfs` name the tools, and the goal template's TCC sentence no longer says the pack has no TCC parser. The wrapper and recipe for `mac_apt.py`, the Keychain route and the six new skills are not in this change (they wait for the pinned reader and the keychain reader).
