### Changed: the Linux CI jobs have room for a slow hosted runner

Over the last 60 CI runs the Linux job (typecheck, node tests, shell suites, UI build) took 36 to 59 min 54 s on success (median 53.5 min) against its 60-minute limit, and four runs were cut off at the limit; on a slow runner `npm test` alone took about 23 minutes and the shell suites were cut off. Its limit is now 90 minutes, as the macOS node parts already have. The Node 22.19 and Node 24 jobs took up to 33 min 54 s and 32 min 24 s of their 35 (two runs of the first were cut off), so both now have 55 minutes. The guard, replay-impact, pack-version and microVM jobs used at most 44 percent of their limits and are unchanged. A hang is still stopped by the 5-minute limit of every test. No test is changed or skipped by this.
