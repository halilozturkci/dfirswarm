### Fixed: cloud-forensics reads past a record over max_record_bytes, keeps every problem of a gzip file, names zero padding apart from damage, and moves the parser labels

In an array or an envelope (`{"Records": [...]}`, `{"value": [...]}`, a bare array), a record over `max_record_bytes` ended the read of the rest of the array: "the rest of the array was not read", and the records after it were neither counted nor listed. cloudtrail_parse, signin_analyse and ual_parse now skip it by scanning to its end without holding it (the scan is under the clock, it steps over strings and counts brackets, and it holds one chunk of the file whatever the record's size; a 1 GiB record was skipped with a peak of 40 MiB), count it as rejected with its record number, line and length, and read the records after it. The file is partial and the reason says so. A record that is both nested past the JSON decoder's depth and over the cap used to end the call with "unexpected failure: TooLong"; it is skipped the same way. If the file, the clock or `max_expanded_bytes` ends inside such a record, the stop says so. The rate of the scan depends on the shape of the record: on the machine it was measured on, 128 MiB of one-character strings took 10 s, 128 MiB of empty objects 30 s, and the 420 s default clock bounds all of them.

Zero bytes after the last gzip member are no longer damage. GNU gzip ignores such padding, and a file with it read as partial ("N bytes follow the end of the compressed stream and are not another member"). They are now counted as `trailing_zero_bytes` in the file's census row and the read stays complete when nothing else is wrong. Zeros followed by anything else, including another member (which GNU gzip would drop), are still reported, and a member cut short is not padding.

Every problem of a gzip file is now kept in order. Until now a later problem replaced an earlier one, so a CRC failure followed by a length failure showed only the second. The two checks are named apart ("member 2 of the compressed stream fails its length check"), the first 25 problems of a file are listed in its census row and the rest are counted ("N more problem(s) of this file were counted and are not listed").

The `parser` label of every record moves from `<tool>/4` to `<tool>/6`. It had not moved since before the foreign-record, long-integer and per-record-isolation changes (5), and records after an oversized one are read now (6), as `docs/packs.md` (a tool's version and its PARSER label) asks. `tests/pack-cloud-shared.test.ts` pins the labels.
