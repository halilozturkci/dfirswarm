### Fixed: the pack counts in `README.md` and `docs/packs.md` are computed, and a test keeps them true

Both documents still said twelve packs, 199 skills, 72 tools and 14 goal templates, and the `README.md` table still carried the counts from before the skills were split into leaves. They now say thirteen packs, as many skills, tools and goal templates as the packs hold, with every row equal to its pack. The new `scripts/pack-counts.ts` computes the counts (the entries of each pack's `skills/INDEX.md`, the tools with a manifest, the goal templates) and `--write` rewrites the numbers in both documents; `tests/pack-doc-counts.test.ts` fails, naming each number, when a document differs from the packs, so the next pack or skill that lands shows up as a one-command fix instead of a stale table.

The "Routers" section of `docs/packs.md` no longer lists the packs that have one (the list left out the virtualization pack, whose router is `router/start`); it says the `Router:` line of a pack's index is the authority. The virtualization and backup pack is now named in the `README.md` sentence that lists what the packs cover, and "twelve" is gone from the sentences of `docs/packs.md` and `docs/quick-start.md` that did not need a number. Documentation and a test only.
