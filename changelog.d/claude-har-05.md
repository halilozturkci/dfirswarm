### Added: computer-forensics-base reads RAR archives (version 4 and 5) through 7zz

`archive_extract` (version 16) sends a file that starts with the RAR 4 signature (`52 61 72 21 1A 07 00`) or the RAR 5 signature (`52 61 72 21 1A 07 01 00`) to `7zz`, 7-Zip's own Linux build, instead of refusing it as unsupported. It lists with `7zz l -slt -ba`, asks for the plain files it listed by name in a list file with wildcards off, reads their bytes from a pipe, splits them by the listing's sizes, and gives each member the same row as a 7z member (status, sizes, CRC check, flags, path). The reader is given no password. An encrypted member is counted and never opened; a link is recorded with its target; an alternate data stream and a file-copy record are `unsupported`. A reader that exits with a status other than 0 while listing or extracting, or a stream longer than the listing, makes the answer `partial` whatever the members say, and the answer carries the reader's own words (for a cut archive: `Unexpected end of archive`). A run with no 7zz on PATH uses the first of `7z`, `7za` and `7zr` and says that build's RAR codec was not checked; with no reader at all the answer is `failed` and names `7zz`. A RAR with a signature of neither version stays refused by name; CAB and ISO are unchanged. The `archive-members` recipe still does not list a RAR.

The base pack (1.15.0) declares `7zz` in `requires/host.json` as a pinned download: 7-Zip 26.05, one URL and sha256 per architecture (amd64 `22cf82fc…`, arm64 `0b71f6cf…`), `redistributable: false`, the licence from 7-Zip's own License.txt (LGPL-2.1-or-later, with the unRAR licence restriction on the RAR code; BSD-3-Clause and BSD-2-Clause for some code). The image recipe installs it without a Dockerfile edit. `tests/image-programs.sh` gains a readiness line: in an image that holds `7zz` it decodes an embedded copy of the RAR 5 fixture, checks its sha256, lists it (4 files, 6 directories) and reads a member's bytes.

What was measured, and what was not: one real RAR 5 archive (`rar5-subdirs.rar` from the rarfile project's test files, ISC licence; which program made it was not checked, 542 bytes; `tests/fixtures/rar/SOURCE.txt`), stored without compression, read by 7zz 26.05, and its copies cut at every length from 8 to 541 bytes (534 runs): none is `complete`, 69 are `failed` (no member listed) and 465 `partial`; the whole file is `complete`. Not measured: a RAR 4 archive, a compressed or solid RAR (the fixture's files are stored, so it does not show that the RAR decompressors work), encrypted or header-encrypted RAR, a multi-volume set, a link or alternate-stream member, a member name with a line break. Those paths are tested only with a stand-in reader script in `tests/test_archive_extract_rar.py`, which says what the tool does with what a reader prints, not what 7zz prints. The 7z route is unchanged: a 7z reader's non-zero exit status is still only recorded, not made partial.
