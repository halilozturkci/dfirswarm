### Added: network-forensics: four skills, for captured secrets, flow records, encrypted DNS, and engines and coverage

- **`evidence/captured-secrets`** (242 tokens) says where authentication material occurs in a capture, a log or an export, that the job runs with `secret_output: true` before any extraction, and that a finding records a locator, the type, the service and what rotation implies: an attempt is not a success, and never the value, a fragment or a hash. It is a `needs` of `sessions/reconstruct`, `capture/carve-from-images` and `logs/web-proxy-firewall`; the longest of the three chains is 1,683 tokens, under the 1,750 warning.
- **`flows/interpret`** (526 tokens) gives the semantics to settle before a flow-record number is used: exporter, observation domain, templates, sampling, timeouts, direction, counter semantics, NAT, and that a sampled volume is a bound. It says this pack provides no flow reader, as `capture/what-you-have` does and the README now does; it names a flow tool only when one exists.
- **`metadata/encrypted-dns`** (510 tokens) covers DoH, DoT and DoQ: identifying the transport is not recovering queries, and "no DNS queries" is wrong when only port 53 was examined. `metadata/dns-tls` loses the encrypted-DNS part of its "no QUIC or encrypted-DNS method yet" clause (the QUIC part stays) and points to the leaf.
- **`analysis/engines-and-coverage`** (509 tokens) names the `dry_run` mode of `zeek_run` and `suricata_run` and what makes an engine run reproducible and what it does not cover.
- The router names each new id once (274 of 400 tokens), the index is 610 of 1,000, the README says "Seventeen skills", and the counts in `README.md` and `docs/packs.md` follow. `logs/web-proxy-firewall` gave up about twenty bytes of wording ("and not for" to "or", "the returned aggregates" to "aggregates") so that its new `needs` entry leaves it at 799 of the 800-token leaf limit.
