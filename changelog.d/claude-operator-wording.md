### Changed: "the operator" means the person who runs the case, in the windows goal and the triage skill

`packs/windows-forensics/goals/intrusion-triage.md` asked for "how the operator first reached this machine" and "every program the operator ran", where an examiner reads the operator as the person running the case; it now says the intruder. The triage skill `verify/manifests.md` asked for "the claimed operator and execution account" of a collection; it now says who the record says ran the collector, and the account it ran under. Nothing else in either sentence changes. `tests/pack-goals-checks.test.ts` gains a guard: no goal says "the operator" next to an intrusion verb (reached, ran, gained, moved, exfiltrated and a few more). A grep of the goals and skills for a destructive command quoted as an attacker's (`vssadmin delete`, `wevtutil cl`, `bcdedit`, `wbadmin delete`, `cipher /w`, `fsutil usn deletejournal`, `Clear-EventLog`, `shred`, `rm -rf`, `history -c`) finds none, so no "recognise, do not run" line is added.
