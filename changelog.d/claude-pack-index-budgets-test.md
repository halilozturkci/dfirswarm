### Added: a suite that holds every pack to the skill index budgets, and a script that prints them

`tests/pack-index-budgets.test.ts` reads every pack under `packs/` with the harness's own `readPackIndex` and `renderSkillsSection` and fails when a pack is added without a router or a leaf is added without the router naming it. For each pack: `INDEX.md` names a router the harness reads, the file resolves and says `router: true` and is the pack's only one, the router is at most 400 tokens and names every other skill of the pack exactly once and no other, the entry lines of `INDEX.md` are exactly the pack's skills and cost at most 1,000 tokens as the harness counts them. For the run: every profile of `images/profiles.json`, and the base pack beside any one, two or three other packs, is shown within 2,500 tokens. Each rule is also proved on a small pack built to break it, so the checks cannot pass by looking at nothing.

`scripts/pack-index-budgets.ts` prints the measurement the suite reads: per pack the entries, index tokens and router; per profile the tokens of every entry, the tokens the prompt shows and the mode; and, for the base pack beside one, two and three others, how many runs fit whole, how many are shown by routers and the largest shown figure (`--json` for the full list).
