### Changed: the triage skills say what a delivery shows and does not show, and no longer claim what its tools do not measure

The five skills are rewritten in the defender's voice as fourteen leaves (five decision-rule leaves and nine second-level leaves they point to: `identify/collector-clues`, `identify/acquisition-mode`, `verify/target-outcomes`, `verify/time-layers`, `normalise/inventory`, `normalise/parsers`, `gaps/coverage-statement`, `plan/sources-windows`, `plan/sources-linux`), each at most 800 tokens (the largest is about 720), each opening with "Use when ... Not for ...", with `needs` at most two deep, and each separating what the artefact shows, what it does not show and what to record. `identify/collector` treats `collection_id` as a hypothesis from names, first bytes and limited log parsing, keeps attribution, acquisition mode and delivery composition apart, and reads a null failure count as "not determined" and a zero as "none in the rows read". `verify/manifests` gives three results (run baseline, manifest agreement, acquisition provenance) instead of one "verified": `check_inputs` compares `inputs/` with the run's own `inputs.json` and never opens a container, and no tool of this pack reconciles a manifest with the files yet; outcome states and the four timestamp layers move to leaves, and a uniform extracted date is a lead, not metadata loss. `normalise/layout` keeps the source, delivered and analysis paths apart, reads `source_path_hypothesis` as a guess and `source_path_observed` as the only collector-backed path, and no longer says "the platform packs work unchanged". `gaps/what-is-missing` is now "What this delivery can and cannot establish": it no longer says there is no unallocated space, no slack, no volume and that every offset command is irrelevant (delivered files can hold deleted records, resident data, free pages and record slack; embedded images and raw exports are looked for before a source is written down as absent), and its invented example report becomes a coverage statement filled only from cited records. `plan/what-to-collect` is "Requesting additional evidence through the operator": the harness never performs live collection, `requires_host: [uac, velociraptor]` is gone because the body commands neither, "memory first" is the examiner's recorded decision and not an order, one clock comparison is not a historical offset, and a configuration is observed state.

`tests/pack-links.pending` loses the pack's five lines, `PACK_LINKS_STRICT=1` reports nothing for it, `CyLR` is a `mentions:` entry, and `uac` and `velociraptor` stay declared with a stated reason in `pack.json` (`unreferenced_ok`) and in `requires/host.json`: operator preparation, and candidate offline readers of their own output. The README and `LICENCE` follow the tools and the pinned downloads. The goal template asks the delivered-object inventory and the integrity and time-provenance questions, asks for the four timestamp layers instead of "whether the timestamps are original", and binds its `unallocated` check to the answer's own section; its three-event minimum is the owner's to decide and is unchanged. `tests/pack-triage-skills.test.ts` holds the size and shape rules, the tool fields the skills name, and the old claims gone. New skills (`coverage/delivered-collection`, `normalise/container-members`, `verify/collector-effects`, `identify/collector-records`, `verify/raw-and-derived`) and the manifest, path-map and coverage tools are Phase 2.
