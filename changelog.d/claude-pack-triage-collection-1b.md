### Changed: the triage skills say what a delivery shows and does not show, and no longer claim what its tools do not measure

The five skills are rewritten in the defender's voice as fourteen leaves (five decision-rule leaves and nine second-level leaves they point to: `identify/collector-clues`, `identify/acquisition-mode`, `verify/target-outcomes`, `verify/time-layers`, `normalise/inventory`, `normalise/parsers`, `gaps/coverage-statement`, `plan/sources-windows`, `plan/sources-linux`), each at most 800 tokens (the largest is about 790), each opening with "Use when ... Not for ...", with `needs` at most two deep, and each separating what the artefact shows, what it does not show and what to record (the request leaf `plan/what-to-collect` records only). `identify/collector` treats `collection_id` as a hypothesis from names, first bytes and limited log parsing, keeps attribution, acquisition mode and delivery composition apart (`mixed` is more than one kind of object, and a note beside an image is another file), and reads `failed_target_count: null` as "not determined" and a zero as "none in the rows read". `verify/manifests` gives three results (run baseline, manifest agreement, acquisition provenance) instead of one "verified": `check_inputs` compares `inputs/` with the run's own `inputs.json` and never opens a container, and no tool of this pack reconciles a manifest with the files yet; outcome states and the four timestamp layers move to leaves, and a uniform extracted date is a lead, not metadata loss. `verify/target-outcomes` no longer turns a target with no row into "not delivered" (the outcome is unknown until the census says whether its files are there). `normalise/layout` keeps the source, delivered and analysis paths apart, reads `source_path_hypothesis` as a guess and `source_path_observed` as the only collector-backed path, says a collector may drop named streams altogether, and no longer says "the platform packs work unchanged". `gaps/what-is-missing` is now "What this delivery can and cannot establish": it no longer says there is no unallocated space, no slack, no volume and that every offset command is irrelevant (delivered files can hold deleted records, resident data, free pages and record slack; embedded images and raw exports are looked for before a source is written down as absent), and its invented example report becomes a coverage statement filled only from cited records, with the `kind=coverage` fields the worker prompt requires (`looked_for` among them) and the no-more-evidence policy of `acquisition_none_why`. `plan/what-to-collect` is "Requesting additional evidence through the operator": the harness never performs live collection, `requires_host: [uac, velociraptor]` is gone because the body commands neither, "start from what you hold" no longer tells the agent not to ask for what is missing, "memory first" is the examiner's recorded decision and not an order, one clock comparison is not a historical offset, and a configuration is observed state. The Windows and Linux source lists keep the concrete paths (`/root`, `/var/log/journal`, `sudoers.d`, the hive, event-log, Prefetch, Amcache, SRUM, PSReadLine and task locations) beside the families. Every leaf that names a tool says to run it as a job with `secret_output: true`. `identify/collector` needs nothing (it would have made a chain of three through `evidence/collections`), and no skill advertises an extractor that no pack ships.

`tests/pack-links.pending` loses the pack's five lines, `PACK_LINKS_STRICT=1` reports nothing for it, `CyLR` is a `mentions:` entry, and `uac` and `velociraptor` stay declared with a stated reason in `pack.json` (`unreferenced_ok`) and in `requires/host.json`: operator preparation (UAC is a shell collector an operator runs on the host to be collected; Velociraptor is also a candidate offline reader of its own containers, unverified and used by no skill). The README, `LICENCE` (none of the pack's programs comes from a package manager) and the repository-wide skill count (116) follow. The goal template asks the delivered-object inventory and the integrity and time-provenance questions, asks for the four timestamp layers instead of "whether the timestamps are original", and binds its `unallocated` check to the answer's own section with a single `awk` that exits at the end of the file (the `sed | grep -q` form failed under `pipefail` for a large report, because `grep -q` exits at the first match); its three-event minimum is the owner's to decide and is unchanged. `tests/pack-triage-skills.test.ts` holds the size and shape rules, a needs chain that fits 2,000 tokens even when the base `evidence/verify` has grown to what the base review makes it, the tool fields the skills name (read from the answers of the tools run on a fixture, not from their source), the old claims gone in any wording, and the goal check run the way the harness runs it on reports of three sizes. New skills (`coverage/delivered-collection`, `normalise/container-members`, `verify/collector-effects`, `identify/collector-records`, `verify/raw-and-derived`) and the manifest, path-map and coverage tools are Phase 2.
