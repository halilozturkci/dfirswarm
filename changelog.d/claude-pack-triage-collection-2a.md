### Added: triage-collection reads a collector's records, joins them to the delivered files and generates what the delivery covers

`collection_manifest_parse` reads KAPE copy, skip and console logs, UAC's `uac.log` and `hash_executables` lists, and a Velociraptor container (`uploads.json`, `results/*.json`, `log.json`) into canonical records, one per row or line. A record keeps what the collector wrote as it wrote it: the source path and the delivered path it claims, the size, the digest under the algorithm the collector used (a dictionary keyed by the algorithm, so no digest is read as another), the collector's times as raw strings, the outcome, the collector's own words for a failure, and the locator (log, row, line) of the raw row. Each adapter is versioned and named in the answer's `adapters`; a record in another shape is `partial` or `unsupported`, kept whole where it is a row, and the run's `failures_recorded.count` is `null`, never 0, unless every failure-source record of the run was read whole, the walk was complete and no archive at the top went unopened. CyLR writes no record this pack has a schema for and is listed as unsupported. The collector's version is `unknown` unless a record states it.

`collection_manifest_verify` joins every delivery claim to the delivered file it names (a KAPE destination by its longest tail under the log's directory, case-folded; a Velociraptor stored name; UAC's `[root]` convention, said per row) and checks the path, the size and the digest apart: `matched`, `mismatch`, `missing`, `unverifiable`, `ambiguous` or `read_failed`, and `unexpected` for a delivered file inside a per-file record's directory that no record names. The collector's digest is compared in its own algorithm and an independent SHA-256 is measured beside it in the same pass. `missing` is said only when the census of the delivery is complete; a skip or an upload error is the collector's outcome and is counted, not checked; a sparse Velociraptor upload is not compared by digest; a link is recorded and never followed. The digests of a file whose name marks a credential store are compared and not printed; with `write_values` in a `secret_output` job they go to the sealed values file.

`collection_path_map` states, for every delivered file, `observed` (a record names it and gives its source path, with the record's locator and how it was joined), `ambiguous`, `hypothesis_only` or `collector_record`; the convention reading is `collection_index`'s own function, copied unchanged and held equal by a test, and sits beside an observation without becoming one. It also states non-UTF-8 and percent-escaped components, NTFS stream relationships, the drive of a source path and the collisions. `collection_coverage` takes questions as case-insensitive globs (no regular expression) over source and delivered paths, optional `requested`, `examined` and the verify tool's `checks` table, and generates its statements only from counts of records and files; an empty match says it is about this delivery and the records read.

The `collection-intake` recipe reads a collector's zip or tar in one pass without extracting: every member with a SHA-256, the collector's records, and one verification row per claim, from the same adapters. An archive inside the archive is named and not opened, a 7z is not read, and a bound that is hit is "could not tell". Five skills (`coverage/delivered-collection`, `normalise/container-members`, `verify/collector-effects`, `identify/collector-records`, `verify/raw-and-derived`) teach them, the router names them, and `identify/collector`, `verify/manifests`, `normalise/layout` and `gaps/coverage-statement` no longer say that no tool reconciles a manifest, that no extractor ships or that other collectors' records are not read. Pack `triage-collection`.

### Changed: the shared withholding block of the two earlier triage tools

`collection_id` and `collection_index` carry the block unchanged except that its comment names every tool that carries it and that the serialisation check treats a field named `sha1` or `md5` that holds exactly 40 or 32 lower-case hex digits as it already treated `sha256` (a digest of 40 or 32 characters has a run of 19 digits in roughly one of every few hundred). Withholding stays by shape and its measured misses are those the manifests state; the new tools add no filter or sort over withheld text. Pack `triage-collection`.
