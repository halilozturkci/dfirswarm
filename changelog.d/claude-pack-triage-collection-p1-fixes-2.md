### Security: triage-collection withholding judges what a reader sees, in linear time, with its residual rates measured

- The withholding of `collection_id` and `collection_index` runs on a copy of the text without NUL, zero-width, bidi and other format characters, combining marks and variation selectors, with full-width forms folded, and withholds the span of the original, so a token split by an invisible character, written in another case, glued to a letter or read as UTF-16 in 8 bits is found. New shapes: the token families of GitLab, Stripe, Google, npm, SendGrid, DigitalOcean, Twilio and others, padded base64, the value after any name that says it is a secret (read back from the `=` or `:`, so `DB_PASSWORD=`, `MYSQL_PWD=`, `adminPassword:` and `"password": "x"` count), cookie headers, the options of curl, wget, smbclient, 7z, rar, zip, redis-cli, ldap and openssl, and random-looking letters and digits (alone or as a `/`-joined key such as an AWS secret key) in a name or path of 16 characters or more and in a token of a line of 20 or more.
- Measured on random plants (1,000 per shape and context): the listed shapes, the unlisted families and the invisible-character splits come out in 0.2% or fewer of the plants; AWS-secret-shaped keys and base64 of 20 to 48 bytes in 0.7% or fewer, base64 of 16 bytes in 2.5%, 20 random letters and digits in 1.5%, 32 in 0.5%, base36 text of 24 to 32 characters in 18% to 26%; a plain password or passphrase alone in a name, a UUID-shaped key, 24 lower-case letters and 16 characters of base32 are printed. The rates are in both manifests and asserted by `tests/pack-triage-rates.test.ts` from above for what is claimed and from below for what is not. Cost on real names, measured: 0.47% of this repository's paths, 0.14% of 37,916 Linux package paths and 3.64% of 57,142 macOS system and application paths (hardware ids and GUID-named profiles read as random) are withheld.
- Linear time: a text over 16 KiB is judged in overlapping windows with the clock read between them (a text the run has no time left to judge is withheld whole), user-info and client options are found by hand, every repetition is bounded, and the cache of judged texts holds at most a few MiB (it held every distinct text: 300 lines of 1 MB took 692 MB). A line of 1 MiB of `a+`, `mysql ` or `//a:` is answered in seconds; before, 150 KB took 34 s to 71 s.
- `contains` of `collection_index` accepts only a restricted language (literals, `.`, classes, `^ $ |` and groups that are not repeated, with at most one unbounded repetition, two `{n,m}`, four `?` and four `|` inside groups, no back-reference or lookaround) and refuses anything else by name, so `(a?){30}a{30}`, `a*a*a*a*a*a*a*b`, `.*.*.*.*.*.*x` and `(a|a)` repeated no longer run for minutes on a long name. The language bounds the number of paths the engine tries, not the time (an accepted pattern can still take minutes on a path of 600 characters), so every search of one name also runs under a clock of 2 seconds that interrupts the regular-expression engine itself: a search that exceeds it ends the run with an error that says so.
- The order of rows, runs, objects, results files and copy logs follows the text as it is printed; names that print alike (the same kind of withheld text of the same length) are ordered by a keyed hash of the raw name under a salt drawn afresh in each run, which is not the listing order, not the inode and not the name's rank, and is not the same in the next run, so the position of a row among its siblings, and the finding ids handed out in that order, tell nothing about a withheld name (under a name-sorted listing the rank of five equal names was read back from the ids with a correlation of -0.5 to -1). The price is that names that print alike come out in a different order in each run.
- A copy-log row belongs to the files whose path relative to the log's directory is the tail of its DestinationFile and whose DestinationFile begins with the prefix the files that name a row in full agree on; a row named by more than one file, or a log whose files name two prefixes equally often, makes its files `ambiguous`, never observed. A stray `$MFT` beside the real `C/$MFT` no longer claims the row.
- `collection_index` lists the copy logs (and the unreadable and unsupported ones) as tables, 25 inline and the whole in a file, so the summary no longer outgrows the first 64 KiB; the observed path cites the line of the copy log as well as the row.
- 26 more credential-bearing names are marked and not hashed: Terraform state, `credentials.xml`, `wp-config.php`, secrets and token files, `.my.cnf`, `.s3cfg`, `.boto`, RDP and OpenVPN profiles, `.vnc/passwd`, `shadow*`, gcloud and Azure token stores, `.p8`, `.jceks`, `.pkcs12`, `.asc`, BitLocker recovery key files, a file named `credentials` anywhere, `lsass.dmp`.
- The user-info budget (4,096 `@` candidates in one text) withholds from the start of the candidate's segment, not from its `@`, so the password of a URL whose `@` is the 4,097th candidate is withheld too.
- The possible original of a renamed stream is made from the name as it is printed (a token that holds `_`, such as a JWT, base64url, `hf_` or `sk-proj-`, was split at its last `_` before it was judged); a results `Error` and a log.json `message` that are not strings are judged with their characters as they are (JSON escapes for NUL, zero-width and bidi characters had hidden a split token); `DEFAULT`, a Velociraptor log level, is a key of `log_levels`.
- Both tools handle SIGINT as they handle SIGTERM and SIGHUP; the time limit is read while a copy log is loaded; a KAPE console log with a line it does not know is `partial`; inline path lists are cut at 256 characters with the whole in the table; a directory listing holds a name and an inode per entry, not a directory entry.
- The manifests declare `secret_output` (`write_values` for `collection_id`; `write_values` and `hash_credential_stores` for `collection_index`) and both tools write a values file, or a credential-store digest, only when the job service ran the job as secret output (`SECRET_OUTPUT=1`).
- Skills: three Sensitive-output sentences say that withholding is by shape; the Velociraptor failed-upload sentence names the version it was checked against by pointing to the tool; the key-id route is stated; the licence labels of Velociraptor and CyLR are recorded as declared and unverified. Tool versions: collection_id 5, collection_index 6; pack 1.6.0.
