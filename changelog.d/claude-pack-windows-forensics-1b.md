### Changed: the Windows pack's skills say what each record shows, what it does not and what corroborates it, in leaves within the size budget

The 24 skills of `windows-forensics` are rewritten against the tools as the first part of this work fixed them and against the worker prompt's rules, and split into 52 leaves (each of the 24 ids is still a skill; 28 leaves are new). The overclaims are gone: adjacent RIDs as a sign of an attacker's account, the Recycle Bin SID as who deleted a file, ShellBags and UserAssist as a person's action, `$LogFile` as an independent wall clock, a gap in record ids as a count of missing events, today's timezone bias applied to a past date, `ProfileList` as a full session history, the absence of a persistence entry as intent, event 5145 as a file read, and an empty channel as a cleared one. In their place each leaf names the observation, what it shows, what it does not and what to record, keeps tool names and Windows build facts to what the pack's code and requirements support (a build, version or retention fact that no source gives is left out or worded "check the build"), and leaves the attack walkthroughs out. The memory leaf takes the corrected injection, strings and YARA rules of the memory pack and names that pack's skills and tools only conditionally.

Every leaf is at most 800 tokens (the largest is 792, `filesystem/mft`) and 300 lines, opens with "Use when ... Not for ...", needs at most two leaves deep (never a base skill), and its index entry is at most 40 tokens; a second-level leaf is opened from its parent "only if" the question calls for it. What the review found repeated is said once, in a home leaf, and pointed to by id elsewhere (hive state, key times, the second-reader rule and the withheld-values rule in `registry/overview` and `registry/readers`; the zone rule in `registry/clock`; LogonId and "an account is not a person" in `accounts/sessions`; the coverage conditions and the record-id gap in `logs/coverage`; the three snapshot states in `filesystem/shadowcopies`; the time-set indicators in `filesystem/timestamps`; the ESE export in `execution/esedb`). The pack's skills total about 34,100 tokens against 35,300 before, with the review's new content added. A new suite, `tests/pack-windows-skills.test.ts`, holds the budget, the shape, the pointers and the cross-references, that every tool field or flag a leaf teaches is one its tool writes or reads, and that the claims the review took apart stay gone.

Each leaf's `tools:` and `requires_host:` list exactly what its body names, a name that belongs to a pack this one cannot depend on is in `mentions:` (`mem_profile`, `mem_fs`, `pwsh`), and a leaf whose tool can return content that may be a secret says to run it as a job with `secret_output: true` and what the tool withholds (by name or place only, in `regkv`'s case). Where the pack provides no reader, the leaf says so instead of implying one: no UserAssist, BAM, ShimCache, SRUM-join, SAM, WMI-repository, bitmap-cache or `$LogFile` decoder, no replay of registry transaction logs, no assembler for split script blocks, no adapter from carved records into a rule engine. Paths a tool writes are given as the agent's own `work/<your id>/...` (a job's run directory is read-only), a shadow-copy store is mounted in the agent's own VM and its files are copied out and sealed before a job reads them, and the date properties of a device are decoded from the little-endian bytes `regkv` returns. `skills/INDEX.md` is regenerated with the new titles; the 68 lines of the golden list `tests/pack-links.pending` that belonged to this pack stay deleted, and `unreferenced_ok` records why `pffexport`, `impacket` and `dpapick3` are declared and named by no skill, the last two pending the owner's decision on credential tooling (their entries are unchanged). Two goal templates' introductions no longer say that `execution/overview` ranks artefacts or that `artifacts/shell` proves what was opened; their questions and checks are untouched. A tool stopped by a signal answers `status: interrupted`, and the three leaves that list a tool's statuses (`execution/esedb`, `filesystem/shadowcopies`, `logs/hunting`) say it is no result.
