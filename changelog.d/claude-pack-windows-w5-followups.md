### Fixed: the windows-forensics registry, event log and ESE readers keep to their time limits, and say so in plain words (windows-forensics)

Follow-ups to the withholding work of `regkv`, `shellbags`, `amcache_apps`, `evtx_query`, `evtx_carve` and `esedb_query`, from the final independent check. The shared boundary block is still byte-equal in the six tools, and the four tool-library twins are re-copied.

- **`regkv` reads its clock for every subkey of the key it is listing.** The clock was read once per key taken from the queue, so one key with several hundred thousand subkeys (a hostile hive of 100 MB or more; about 37 microseconds each with the boundary) ran past the tool's own 20 seconds, past the seal stop and past the 30 seconds the manifest allows, and the answer was lost. The subkeys not reached are counted (`subkeys_not_listed_for_time`), not listed or judged, and the key being listed is named once under `stopped_branches` with the number it left. Measured on a hive of 60,000 subkeys of one key with the tool's clock set to 1 second: 2.5 seconds before (all 60,000 listed after the time was up), 1.1 seconds after (2,859 listed, 57,141 counted).
- **`shellbags` no longer lists every sibling after its time limit.** Past the limit each sibling and each numbered value without a key still cost a decoded, sealed entry and a sealed row naming it. The first 1,000 (`MAX_UNOPENED_LISTED`, as in `regkv`) are named and listed and the rest are counted (`not_walked_not_named`, `entries_not_listed_for_time`) and reflected in `status_basis`. Measured on a BagMRU of 60,000 siblings with the clock set to 1 second: 5.3 seconds before, 1.3 seconds after.
- **`tree_complete` means what it says.** It was true together with `walk_stopped_by_time_limit: true` and unopened branches whenever fewer than 2,000 keys had been listed. It is now true only when every key reached is in the inline listing and the walk was not stopped by its time limit; the manifest defines it, and "the inline listing holds the first 2,000 keys" in `status_basis` is now decided by the number of keys listed, not by this flag.
- **`amcache_apps` names the layout it stopped in with fixed words.** The stop was labelled with the name of the inventory key, a name from the hive, so on the late path (past the judging time) `status_basis` became a placeholder and the reader could not tell why the answer was partial. It now says "the newer layout (InventoryApplicationFile)", "the older layout (File), between its volumes" or "the older layout (File), inside a volume", and the line is one of the tool's own words.
- **The last-row memo of `row_object` keeps `1`, `True` and `1.0` apart** (and `0`, `False`, `0.0`, `-0.0`): they compare equal, so a paging-file row after `{"a": 1}` could come out with `1` for `true`. The type is part of the key. Same text in all six tools.
- **The seal stop is 3.5 seconds before the manifest's time limit** (was 2): the string being judged when it passes is finished first, and one string of 256 KiB takes up to 2.3 seconds on a loaded machine (`a:b:c@`, the worst measured). The tools' own clocks of `shellbags` (114 seconds), `amcache_apps` (84) and `evtx_query` (54) move earlier by 1 to 3 seconds so that each still ends at least 1.5 seconds before its seal stop; the others are unchanged (`regkv` 20 s against 26.5, `evtx_carve` 590 against 596.5, `esedb_query` 285 against 296.5). A test reads the constants and the manifests.
- **Past the judging time a short plain string in a typed field is checked against the grammar of its type.** A SID, GUID, digest, MAC, address, time, number or version field holding a word of 15 characters or fewer was printed unchecked on the late path; it now has to fit its type like on the normal path (three characters or fewer are still shown).

`tests/pack-windows-forensics-withholding-followups.test.ts` covers each item with a patched clock (a counter, or a clock that starts at its first reading) and in-process driving of the block in all six tools; every case fails on the previous tools. `logs/security.md` is untouched (798.1 of 800 tokens).
