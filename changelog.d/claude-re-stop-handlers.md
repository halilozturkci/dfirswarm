### Fixed: every reverse-engineering tool and recipe answers a stop signal once, from the moment its handlers are installed at module level

A SIGTERM, SIGINT or SIGHUP that arrived before `main()` had run its first lines ended `doc_probe`, `entropy_map`, `pe_info`, `doc_structure` and `pe_directories` and the `capa-floss` and `static-binary` recipes with no answer at all (the signal killed the interpreter), and `doc_probe`, `entropy_map` and `pe_info` had no stop handling of any kind: a signal at any moment gave no JSON. Now `doc_probe`, `entropy_map` and `pe_info` carry the windows lineage's shared stop block (the same text as `regkv`, `shellbags` and `amcache_apps`, held by `tests/pack-shared-stop.test.ts`) right after their imports, print their answers through its `print_answer` (which holds the stop signals back while the answer is written, so exactly one complete document is written), and set their own last word in `main()`: `entropy_map`'s names the profile it leaves and how far the pass got. `fuzzy_hash` and the two recipes install the shared process block's handlers right after the block, at module level, with a minimal last word that `main()` replaces; the recipes' last word now also prints their answer (status `interrupted`) beside the receipt, and `capa-floss` removes a receipt being written before it writes the last one, so no hidden temporary file is left. `doc_structure` and `pe_directories` install their stop handlers at module level, right after the function that answers (with the flags it reads defined before it); the SIGALRM hard limit is still armed only by `main()` with its deadline.

Every carrier answers once and exits 128 plus the signal for each of the three signals, right after the line that installs its handlers and at seeded random moments of a real run (`tests/pack-re-stop.test.ts`). The interpreter's own start-up and the compiling of the file are not claimed. Tools `doc_probe` 7, `pe_info` 5, `entropy_map` 5, `fuzzy_hash` 7, `doc_structure` 3, `pe_directories` 2; recipes `capa-floss` 1.0.4 and `static-binary` 1.1.3. Pack `reverse-engineering`.
