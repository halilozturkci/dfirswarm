### Added: windows-forensics reads UserAssist, BAM, DAM and ShimCache (`userassist_bam_shimcache`)

One tool reads UserAssist (NTUSER.DAT), BAM and DAM (SYSTEM) and ShimCache / AppCompatCache (SYSTEM) into records, each with its raw bytes (`raw_hex`) beside the decoded fields, the `layout` and the `layout_basis`, and `unsupported` with the bytes kept when a value or an entry does not fit a known layout (UserAssist values of 72 and 16 bytes, BAM and DAM values of 24 bytes, ShimCache in the Windows 7 32-bit and 64-bit, 8.0, 8.1 and 10 layouts). The decoding is the tool's own, from the published layouts, and the offsets are not checked against real hives; the regipy plugins are not used, and no second reader (RECmd, RegRipper) cross-checks the records: `cross_check` is always `not_run`. A UserAssist hive does not name its owner, so `sid` is null unless `owner_sid` is passed. The filters `contains` and `sid` look only in the text the answer prints, and a record not matched whose printed text holds a withheld part makes the answer partial (`contains_blind_records`). Every answer states the interpretation limits (presence is not execution, a ShimCache time is not an execution time, a user whose hive was not collected is not a user with no entry). `tests/pack-windows-forensics-userassist-bam-shimcache.test.ts` is the new suite.

### Changed: the windows-forensics skills and the recipe no longer say the pack has no UserAssist, BAM, DAM or ShimCache reader

`execution/userassist`, `execution/shimcache` and `registry/readers` name `userassist_bam_shimcache` (and say its offsets are unverified and that nothing cross-checks it). The `windows-artefacts` recipe does not run the new tool; it keeps dissect.target's output of the `userassist`, `bam` and `shimcache` functions as a second reading, and its `not_covered` list and `recipe.json` exclusions say so.
