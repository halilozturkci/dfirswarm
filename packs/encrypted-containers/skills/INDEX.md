# Skills in this pack

Fetch a body with `skill("<id>")`. A body may name others; fetch those the same way.

Router: `encrypted/router`

- `archives/formats` ZIP, 7-Zip, RAR, Office and PDF protection: You read a member's protection, a ZIP time, an Office or a PDF encryption structure.
- `archives/protected` Examining encrypted archives and protected documents: An archive or document may require a password.
- `encrypted/router` Encrypted-container notes by question: Pick an encrypted-container note when none is listed, or you cannot choose.
- `identify/by-format` Encryption signs by format, and their limits: You name BitLocker, LUKS, Apple, VeraCrypt, archive, Office or PDF protection from a signature.
- `identify/headers` Identifying encrypted evidence and its limits: A volume, archive or document may be encrypted.
- `keys/other-sources` Password stores, memory, escrow and shell history: Key material may sit in a store, keychain, memory image, escrow record or shell history.
- `keys/recovery-key-scan` What recovery_key_scan shows and does not show: You run recovery_key_scan over files, an image or a capture and report what it found.
- `keys/where-they-hide` Locating and safeguarding authorised key material: A case question requires access to encrypted evidence.
- `recovery/authorised` Authorising and running password_recover: The case contract authorises an attempt to recover a password from evidence or an extracted hash.
- `volumes/bitlocker` BitLocker protectors and authorised examination: A Windows volume may use BitLocker.
- `volumes/bitlocker-opening` Opening a BitLocker volume with supplied material: The case supplies a recovery password, key file or escrow export to open the volume.
- `volumes/filevault-apfs` FileVault and APFS volumes: An Apple volume is encrypted: legacy Core Storage FileVault 2 or APFS.
- `volumes/luks` LUKS volumes, headers and keyslots: A volume, image or detached header may be LUKS.
- `volumes/luks-filevault` Choosing the LUKS or FileVault examination route: A Linux or Apple volume appears encrypted.
