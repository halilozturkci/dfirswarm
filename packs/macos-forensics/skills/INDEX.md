# Skills in this pack

Fetch a body with `skill("<id>")`. A body may name others; fetch those the same way.

Router: `macos/router`

- `accounts/attribution-remote` Attribution limits and remote access on a Mac: Tie an action to an account or a person, or decide whether a session was remote.
- `accounts/users` Accounts, sessions and attribution limits: Relate activity to accounts and distinguish local from remote sessions.
- `artifacts/fsevents` FSEvents, retained filesystem change records: Assess filesystem changes and relative order without inventing timestamps.
- `artifacts/fsevents-dating` Ordering and dating FSEvents records: Order FSEvents records, date one, read its flags, or use an external volume's log.
- `artifacts/knowledgec` KnowledgeC activity records and their limits: Interpret retained application and device-state activity.
- `artifacts/knowledgec-fields-time` KnowledgeC row fields and time columns: Read the values, metadata and dates of a KnowledgeC row from knowledgec_query.
- `artifacts/plists` Property lists and structured archives: Read persisted values while preserving types, provenance and secret boundaries.
- `artifacts/plists-archives-dates` Keyed archives, plist dates, recent items: A plist is a keyed archive, holds a date-like number, or lists recent items.
- `filesystem/apfs` APFS, snapshots, and what deletion means now: Establish acquired volumes, snapshots, timestamps and recovery limits.
- `filesystem/apfs-encryption` APFS encryption, FileVault and hardware limits: An APFS volume is unreadable or FileVault state is in question.
- `filesystem/apfs-recovery` APFS deletion, recovery limits and timestamps: Say what a deleted APFS file leaves behind, or read APFS file times.
- `logs/unified` The unified log: Examine retained diagnostic and operational events with their decoding and coverage limits.
- `logs/unified-readers` Unified log readers, staging and retention levels: unified_log fails or is partial, or the choice of reader or its version is in question.
- `macos/router` macOS notes by question: Pick one when none is listed, or you cannot choose.
- `persistence/login-items-quieter` Login items, background items and the quieter routes: A sweep reaches login items, cron, profiles, extensions or privacy permissions.
- `persistence/mechanisms` Where something arranges to run again: Distinguish persistence configuration, registration, enabled state and execution.
- `security/keychains` macOS keychains, identification and opening: A keychain file is in the evidence, or an answer needs what a Mac's keychain held.
- `triage/clocks-hardware` Zone, epochs and hardware generation of the Mac: Convert a local time, or say which Mac hardware generation the evidence shows.
- `triage/system-profile` Build the system profile before anything else: Establish the acquisition, OS build, hardware evidence, clocks and acquired volumes.
