# Skills in this pack

Fetch a body with `skill("<id>")`. A body may name others; fetch those the same way.

Router: `mobile/router`

- `android/artifacts` Android artefacts by build, user and protection state: The evidence holds Android application or system data.
- `apps/databases` App databases, transaction state, deleted fragments: An answer rests on an app's SQLite records or on deleted content.
- `apps/fragments` Reading a sqlite_freespace answer: You ran sqlite_freespace and must report what its fragments are.
- `apps/identity-containers` App identity across containers, packages and restores: You must say which app, user and device a directory or package belongs to.
- `apps/messaging` Messaging stores, and what a message row proves: A question rests on Messages, SMS, MMS, RCS, WhatsApp, Signal or Telegram records.
- `extractions/backup-detail` iOS backups and Android .ab files: The evidence is an iOS backup directory or an adb backup.
- `extractions/backups` Local and cloud backups, and what they cover: A backup, cloud copy, transfer or app export is the evidence, or is said to hold the data.
- `extractions/protection-state` Protected data and acquisition boundaries: Data is missing, unreadable or encrypted: a gap or an absence?
- `extractions/what-you-have` Mobile extraction scope and authority: Start here: a phone, backup or app export, before any question.
- `ios/artifacts` iOS artefacts by build and source: The evidence holds iPhone or iPad application or system data.
- `ios/biome-segb` Biome and SEGB records: An activity question depends on an iOS Biome stream.
- `ios/containers-and-time` iOS containers and time conversion: You must attribute an app container or convert an iOS time.
- `ios/knowledgec-activity` KnowledgeC on iOS, and what its events support: An activity question depends on an iOS knowledgeC.db or its streams.
- `ios/unified-logs` iOS unified logs on Linux: An iOS question depends on retained diagnostic logs.
- `location/sources` Mobile location records and uncertainty: The question is where a device, media item or person was.
- `media/provenance` Media originals, derivatives and capture time: A photo or video is cited, and you must say whether you hold the original and when it was made.
- `mobile/router` Mobile notes by question: Pick a mobile note when none is listed, or you cannot choose.
- `validation/parser-coverage` Validating a mobile parser result: A LEAPP or other parser result will back a finding, and you must say what it covered.
