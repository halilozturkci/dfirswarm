# Skills in this pack

Fetch a body with `skill("<id>")`. A body may name others; fetch those the same way.

- `beacons/periodicity` Finding the thing that calls home: You suspect command and control, or a first pass found nothing.
- `capture/carve-from-images` Recovering packet records from disk or memory: No capture was supplied, but a memory image or disk image may retain packet buffers.
- `capture/what-you-have` What the capture can and cannot contain: The evidence includes a pcap, a pcapng, or flow logs.
- `correlate/host` Tying the wire to the machine: You have both a capture and a host image, which is when either becomes conclusive.
- `exfil/volume` Whether data left, and how much: The question is exfiltration rather than intrusion.
- `logs/web-proxy-firewall` Working web, proxy and firewall logs: The evidence is line-oriented network logs rather than a packet capture.
- `metadata/dns-tls` What you can still prove when the payload is encrypted: The traffic is TLS, which is to say most of it.
- `sessions/reconstruct` Putting a session back together: You need what was actually sent, not just who spoke to whom.
