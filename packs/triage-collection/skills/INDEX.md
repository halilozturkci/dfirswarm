# Skills in this pack

Fetch a body with `skill("<id>")`. A body may name others; fetch those the same way.

Router: `triage/router`

- `coverage/delivered-collection` From delivered objects to question coverage: You must say what a delivery covers for a question, before a negative or a count of "all".
- `gaps/coverage-statement` The coverage statement for one question: An answer is a negative or a completeness claim.
- `gaps/what-is-missing` What this delivery can and cannot establish: You write up or rely on anything drawn from a delivery.
- `identify/acquisition-mode` Live, mounted, snapshot or earlier: what was read: Time, consistency or collector contamination of a file matters.
- `identify/collector` Naming the collector and what its records say: A delivery is a tree or an archive from a collector.
- `identify/collector-clues` Layout clues for the collector families: You describe a family's layout, or collection_id names none.
- `identify/collector-records` What a collector's records say: You rely on a collector's own copy, skip, upload or log records.
- `normalise/container-members` Container identity when members become files: A delivery is an archive, or a tree extracted from one.
- `normalise/inventory` What the census guarantees and what it does not: You quote a file count or say a file is not in the delivery.
- `normalise/layout` Source path, delivered path and analysis path: Before you cite a delivered file by path.
- `normalise/parsers` Handing delivered files to the platform parsers: A delivered file goes to a parser.
- `plan/sources-linux` Linux sources to request, by question: You request more evidence from a Linux source.
- `plan/sources-windows` Windows sources to request, by question: You request more evidence from a Windows source.
- `plan/what-to-collect` Requesting additional evidence through the operator: A material question needs evidence that the delivery does not contain.
- `triage/router` Triage-collection notes by question: Pick a triage-collection note when none is listed, or you cannot choose.
- `verify/collector-effects` Collector footprint and chronology: Events may be the collector's own, or the order of collection steps matters.
- `verify/manifests` Baseline, manifest and provenance of a delivery: You take custody of a collection or rely on its manifest.
- `verify/raw-and-derived` Raw artefacts, companions and derived reports: A delivery mixes collected artefacts with parsed output.
- `verify/target-outcomes` What a collector did and did not copy, target by target: A skip, error or result log exists.
- `verify/time-layers` Four timestamp layers in a delivery: File times matter, or many files share one date.
