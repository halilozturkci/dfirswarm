# Skills in this pack

Fetch a body with `skill("<id>")`. A body may name others; fetch those the same way.

Router: `router/start`

- `backup/catalogues-and-retention` Catalogue, repository and retention: A backup system's catalogue, job records, repository or retention setting is evidence.
- `backup/deletion-vs-loss` Deletion attempted, reported, observed, verified: A backup, snapshot or restore point may have been deleted, pruned, expired or overwritten.
- `control-plane/chronology` Clocks across hypervisor, backup and guest: Events from a hypervisor, a management server, a backup system and a guest must be ordered.
- `evidence/boundaries` Planes of virtualization evidence, and handling: You are about to open VM images, hypervisor or backup exports and must scope and protect them.
- `hypervisor/activity` What to ask of hypervisor management records: A VM was powered off, deleted, unregistered, snapshotted, reconfigured or its datastore changed.
- `hypervisor/inventory` What VM configuration files and inventories list: VM configuration files, an inventory or a datastore listing name VMs, disks and snapshots.
- `recovery/survivability` Which recovery points survive, and could one be restored?: The question is whether a VM or dataset can still be recovered, and from what.
- `router/start` Where to start with virtualization and backup evidence: A case involves virtual machine images, a hypervisor, a backup system or its repository.
- `snapshots/chain-integrity` Is the disk or snapshot chain whole?: A VM's disk is a snapshot, delta or differencing disk, or a backing chain must be established.
- `snapshots/header-markers` Header, descriptor and parent markers: snapshot_chain_check lists a header, descriptor, parent or second-reading marker.
- `snapshots/markers` Consolidation and unclean-close markers: snapshot_chain_check lists a marker, or snapshots may have been removed, merged or interrupted.
